Simac is a cloud-native application protection platform built for the enterprise. It unifies posture, workload, identity, and data security across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud, so your team can fix the risks that matter first.

Cloud security teams are drowning in point tools and alerts that lack context.

Posture findings live in one console, workload vulnerabilities in another, and identity risk in a spreadsheet. Simac brings them together in one security graph that spans every account, subscription, and project, and shows you how attackers could actually chain them together.

The platform

Complete cloud protection, from code to runtime

Everything a modern CNAPP should do, built on a single data model instead of stitched-together acquisitions.

Cloud security posture

Continuously detect misconfigurations and drift across AWS, Azure, and GCP, mapped to CIS, NIST, PCI DSS, and your own policies.

Workload protection

Agentless scanning of VMs, containers, and serverless functions for vulnerabilities, malware, and exposed secrets.

Identity & entitlements

Find over-privileged identities, toxic permission combinations, and unused access across every cloud and identity provider.

Data security posture

Discover and classify sensitive data in buckets, databases, and warehouses, and see exactly who and what can reach it.

Shift-left with IaC

Catch risky Terraform, CloudFormation, Bicep, and Helm changes in the pull request, before they reach production.

Attack path analysis

Correlate exposure, vulnerabilities, identities, and data into real attack paths, ranked by business impact.

How it works

From connected to prioritized in minutes

  1. 01

    Connect

    Onboard entire AWS organizations, Azure tenants, and GCP organizations with read-only, agentless access. Nothing to install on your workloads.

  2. 02

    Correlate

    Simac builds a live security graph of your resources, identities, network paths, and data, normalized across providers.

  3. 03

    Remediate

    Act on a short list of critical risks with guided fixes, ticketing integrations, and pull requests sent to the teams who own the code.

Built for the enterprise

Designed for scale, governance, and your existing workflows

Simac is built for security organizations that manage thousands of cloud accounts across business units, regions, and regulatory regimes.

  • SSO & SCIM with SAML and OIDC identity providers
  • Granular RBAC scoped to business units, clouds, and accounts
  • Data residency options to keep findings in your region
  • Audit logging for every user and API action
  • Open API plus integrations with SIEM, SOAR, and ticketing tools
  • Compliance reporting mapped to industry frameworks out of the box
